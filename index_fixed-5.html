Lagos State Unified Examination
Broadsheet System

A free, secure digital broadsheet tool designed specifically for Lagos State Universal Basic Education Board (LASUBEB) teachers. Manage class results, calculate averages, rank pupils, and generate score distribution reports — all from one easy-to-use platform.

Everything a LASUBEB Teacher Needs

Built to match the official LASUBEB unified examination master sheet format.

📝

Smart Score Entry

Enter scores quickly with auto-advance between cells. Supports all subjects across Primary, Nursery, and Kindergarten classes.

🏆

Auto Ranking & Sorting

Pupils are automatically ranked and sorted by average score. Tied pupils receive the same position using standard competition ranking (1, 2, 2, 4).

📊

Score Distribution Report

Generates the official LASUBEB score distribution sheet showing how many pupils scored in each range (0–9, 10–19 … 90–100) per subject.

📅

Three-Term Support

Manages 1st, 2nd, and 3rd term results with automatic Brought Forward (B/F) calculation for cumulative averages across terms.

🔒

Secure Teacher Accounts

Each teacher has a private account. Data is saved automatically and only accessible after login. Admin panel for school management.

🖨️

Print & Export

Print a clean, professional broadsheet directly from your browser. Download a backup of your data anytime and restore it on any device.

How It Works

1

Register Free

Create your teacher account with your email address in seconds.

2

Set Up Your Class

Enter your school name, class, session and customise your subject list.

3

Enter Scores

Type in pupil names and scores. Averages, ranks and remarks update automatically.

4

Print & Submit

Print your broadsheet and score distribution sheet ready for submission.

Who Is This Tool For?

Lagos Broadsheet is built for class teachers, head teachers, and examination officers in Lagos State primary and pre-primary schools operating under the LASUBEB unified examination system.
