Privacy Policy

How Lagos Broadsheet handles your data

📅 Last updated: January 2025  |  Effective date: January 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Lagos Broadsheet ("we", "our", or "the tool"), available at lagosbroadsheet.edu.ng, collects, uses, and stores information when you use our service. By using Lagos Broadsheet, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Lagos Broadsheet collects the following types of information:

  • Account information: When you register, we store your email address, a hashed (encrypted) version of your password, and your school name (used for password recovery). Your actual password is never stored.
  • Broadsheet data: Pupil names, admission numbers, examination scores, class information, school name, LGEA, and session data that you enter into the tool.
  • Usage data: Basic browser information may be collected by our hosting provider and advertising partners for analytics and ad delivery purposes.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

All broadsheet data — including pupil records, scores, and account credentials — is stored in your browser's localStorage. This means:

  • Data is stored locally on your device, not on an external server.
  • Data is only accessible from the browser and device where it was entered.
  • Data will be lost if you clear your browser's site data or cache.
  • We recommend using the Download Backup feature regularly to save a copy of your data.

We do not operate a database server that stores your pupil or examination data. We do not have access to the scores, names, or results you enter.

3. Advertising

Lagos Broadsheet displays advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks including Adsterra and PopAds. These networks may:

  • Use cookies and similar tracking technologies to serve relevant advertisements.
  • Collect non-personally identifiable information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited.
  • Use this information to show you interest-based advertisements on this and other websites.

We do not share your account information, pupil data, or examination results with any advertising network. Advertising cookies are separate from your broadsheet data.

You may opt out of interest-based advertising by visiting your ad network's opt-out page or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings.

4. Cookies

Lagos Broadsheet itself does not use cookies for its core functionality. However, third-party advertising partners may set cookies in your browser. These cookies are governed by the privacy policies of the respective advertising networks.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties except as described below:

  • Advertising partners: Non-personally identifiable browsing data may be shared with ad networks for ad delivery as described in Section 3.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal processes.

6. Children's Data

Lagos Broadsheet is a tool for teachers. Pupil names and scores entered into the tool are stored locally on the teacher's device and are not accessible to us. Teachers are responsible for ensuring that pupil data is handled in accordance with applicable data protection regulations in Nigeria, including the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR).

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information stored within the tool:

  • Passwords are hashed before storage and are never stored in plain text.
  • The tool is served over HTTPS to protect data in transit.
  • Access to broadsheet data requires a valid login.

However, as data is stored locally in the browser, the security of your data also depends on the security of your device and browser.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data: All data you enter is visible to you in the tool at all times.
  • Delete your data: You can clear all your data by clearing your browser's localStorage for this site, or by contacting us to have your account removed.
  • Export your data: Use the Download Backup feature to export your data at any time.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of Lagos Broadsheet after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us through our Contact page.
